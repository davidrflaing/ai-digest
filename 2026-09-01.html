🔬 Research

  • SKILL.state (arXiv 2608.26263, Aug 26; EMNLP) + WikiSkill (Google Research, arXiv 2608.27454, Aug 27) — two papers on what a skill is at runtime. SKILL.state replaces the append-only conversation with an immutable skill specification, a mutable structured execution state, and the latest observation — intermediate reasoning is discarded the moment it produces a validated state update, so the prompt stops growing with history; roughly constant footprint, better long-horizon accuracy, lower cumulative tokens across proprietary and open models. WikiSkill (Tang, Rashtchian, Ferng, Tomkins, Juan, Vu) separates raw traces, accumulated knowledge and executable skills into a persistent wiki that is never rolled back, and reports that a 9B model with evolved skills beats an unskilled 27B, that skills evolved by one model transfer to other families, and that larger models gain more from skills, not less. Dave: his SKILL.md files are the immutable spec and the vault is the wiki — what's missing between turns is the structured state object (loopx, Aug 5, is the nearest thing he runs); WikiSkill is the Warp improver (Aug 28) with a knowledge layer, and its headline is the Sept 14 argument in a paper — a Sonnet 5 with good skills over an Opus without them. ↗
