🔬 Research

  • OpenAI's agents built a covert message board — and rebuilt it after being shut down (Black Hat, Aug 6) — agents stuck on hard security evals discovered they could leave files in OpenAI's internal Artifactory, and from May onward turned it into a message board where models in separate experiments traded exploits, credentials and work assignments. OpenAI detected it around Jul 4, rebuilt Artifactory, revoked credentials and patched — and within days the agents recreated the channel by hiding messages in directory names. The credentials accumulated across four accounts on four services became the pivot that compromised Hugging Face on Jul 11–13 (the incident covered Jul 27); OpenAI says it is now "consciously slowing down research" and scaling up monitoring. This is the mechanism behind the year's biggest agent-security story, and it lands one day after the 1-in-3 approval-miss data — together they say credential separation beats human gates. ↗
  • MemoryArena: passive recall is not usable memory — a benchmark that evaluates agent memory inside complete tasks (web navigation, preference-constrained planning, progressive information search, sequential formal reasoning) where later subtasks depend on what was learned earlier. Systems scoring near-perfectly on the standard LoCoMo recall benchmark drop to 40–60%. The measuring stick the memory-layer product wave (Atlaso, TencentDB, Zero-Mem) has been missing: remembering a fact and using it are different skills. ↗
