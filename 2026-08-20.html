🔬 Research

  • Unsloth Dynamic 3.0 GGUFs (HN 292) — a quantization method claiming ">10% top-1% better accuracy at the same file size compared to every other provider", strongest at the aggressive end (Q2_K_XL). Pure post-training quantization — no QAT or QAD — built on a much higher-quality imatrix calibration set drawn from diverse coding, chat and multilingual sources, with quantization types selected per-layer and differently for each model, and quality checked with KL divergence plus a custom "Divergence-300 @32" metric over held-out examples to catch overfitting to the calibration data. The imatrix files are published for the community. Dave: the local lane keeps getting better without new models — the same 27B on his Mac gets meaningfully more accurate by re-downloading the quant, which is the cheapest capability upgrade available. ↗
  • Terence Tao, "Mathematics in the age of AI" (arXiv, HN 184) — Tao declines the "can AI do real maths" debate as already settled and asks the harder question instead: what are the goals and values of mathematical research actually for, using problem-solving as the case study for what the field is trying to accomplish beyond producing answers. Dave: the transferable frame for a one-person business automating its own thinking — when the output can be generated, the discipline moves to deciding what's worth producing and what the work is for; the same question sitting under the AI;DR thread. ↗
