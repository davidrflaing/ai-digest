  • Polylane, "Sub-agents are just wrong" (Sept 14): 18 agents replaced by one, median time-to-PR 2.2 hours → 35 minutes — the old pipeline was triage, a coordinator, up to 15 hypothesis sub-agents and a coding agent; the new one is a single agent from evidence to pull request.
