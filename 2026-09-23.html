Sales / ops / automation AI

  • [ops] Opus 5.5's guide says unattended agents may stop after posting a status update, and gives the fix (Anthropic docs, Sept 22) — "some of those updates end the turn with text rather than a tool call… An unattended agent loop that treats such a turn as the end of the task stops running there." The fix: keep a checklist, and when a turn ends with items still open and no blocker named, send one message naming them. "Stop after two or three automatic continuations" so a stuck run still ends.
