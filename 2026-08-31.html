🔬 Research

  • "Continuous diffusion language models" (Sander Dieleman, DeepMind; Aug 24; HN 116) — after discrete diffusion won 2023–25, continuous methods are back for one reason: they distil — flow-map methods give single- and few-step sampling that keeps token correlations (discrete diffusion's conditional-independence assumption breaks exactly there), the frameworks are simpler (linear interpolation, not score matching), and few-step models unlock reward-based steering and fine-tuning; the 2026 wave is Categorical / Discrete Flow Maps, LangFlow, Spherical and Hyperspherical Flows, LDLM, ELF, CoBit, RePlaid.
