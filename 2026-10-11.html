🔬 Research

  • "AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance" (Brian Christian, UC Berkeley CHAI, with CMU, MIT, Oxford and UCLA; COLM 2026; Berkeley News Oct 9; HN 38 / 51) — three randomised trials with 1,222 online participants; one group had ChatGPT for fraction problems or SAT reading until it was removed at problem 12, after which their accuracy fell "almost immediately" and skip rates jumped while the control group held steady. Christian: "You almost couldn't ask for a clearer story." Flags: no effect sizes in the press piece, two task types, short exposure. Ten minutes of outsourced effort and the willingness to stay in a hard problem drops: the research shape behind portal tools that make the person do the practice rather than doing it for them. ↗ · Berkeley
  • Memento 3 (UCL and Huawei Noah's Ark Lab; arXiv 2610.11794) — clears all 25 public ARC-AGI-3 games (100.0 mean RHAE) with the language model frozen, using 44% of the human action budget, against 40.7 for an Opus 5 baseline; all the learning lives in a Markdown rulebook plus a compiled Python world-model it rewrites when its predictions fail. Flags: the authors' numbers, reported secondhand. The shape of the plugins' :learn then :consolidate loop again, three days after Harvey's wake-sleep agent. ↗
