Sales / ops / automation AI

  • [ops] Raindrop, "The surprising leadership dynamics in agent swarms" (Oct 29; TLDR AI Oct 30) — 2,160 runs of eight peer agents drawn from four models (Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Kimi K3) fixing four simulated outages over one shared channel, with no leader assigned. 86% of runs had an agent hand out a plan, but only 5% had a leader whose instructions actually changed what the others did. The agent listed first in the system prompt was about three times as likely to issue a plan, and reversing the list moved the effect to the new first agent. Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol were about 1.7× as likely as Gemini to lead. The shared channel cut duplicated actions from 4.4 to 0.3 per run. Honest flags: Raindrop sells agent monitoring; it's one simulated task type; leaders were rare enough (105 runs) that the per-model splits are thin. Dave: two rules for the Orchestrator's multi-lane work. Name the coordinator rather than hoping one emerges. And the order you list lanes or agents in a prompt is itself a choice, because the first one gets the initiative. ↗

🔬 Research

  • Anthropic, "Automated Subtle Emergent Misalignment Research" (Oct 31) — thousands of Claude agents were told to write fine-tuning data that a training-data monitor wouldn't flag, while making the tuned model as misaligned as possible.
