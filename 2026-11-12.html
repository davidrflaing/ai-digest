GitHub repos & OSS tools

  • [repo] Nod (n8n-io/nod; Apache 2.0; n8n Labs "research preview", Nov 10; Product Hunt #2, Nov 11) — a personal agent that runs on your Mac or your own server, with your own model key, and "works in your apps and on websites until the job is finished". Flags: it "doesn't run while your computer is asleep"; the "98% of 678 runs across 226 errands" figure is n8n's own, in a "simulated test world"; commenters asked about permissions and audit logs and this run found no answer. If you want to see what it can do, give it one personal errand with no accounts connected. ↗ · repo
  • [repo] herdr (herdrdev/herdr; Apache-2.0; 50.5k★, +2.2k this week; brew install herdr) — "the runtime your coding agents live on": a terminal multiplexer that marks each pane working, blocked or idle, detaches without stopping work, and lets agents drive it through a CLI and socket. Flag: after a restart it restores the layout, but "the original processes do not survive". Dave: don't switch from cmux. The one idea worth taking is the pane state read from the agent itself: the 🎯 Orchestrator reads screens to tell blocked from idle, and that is where "his typing" errors came from. ↗
