🔬 Research

  • "Why your local LLM feels dumber than it is" (Level1Techs, HN 384) — a measured teardown showing local models underperform their published numbers because of compounding precision loss in the serving stack, not model quality: different CUDA kernels, quantisation schemes, KV-cache settings and tensor-parallel configs produce measurably different token probabilities and sometimes flip the chosen token. Ranked by top-1 agreement with the baseline: W8A16 (INT8) best, official FP8 acceptable, AWQ W4A16 weaker, NVFP4 worst at ~50% disagreement by 88k context; INT4 KV-cache quantisation reproducibly breaks tool calling; temperature set too low produces infinite loops. Recommendations: match the model card's sampler settings exactly, keep the KV cache in BF16 where you can, and test with 100k-context multi-tool workloads rather than zero-shot prompts. Dave: the checklist to run before deciding a local model "can't do" a job — omlx (yesterday) gives him the server, this tells him which knobs silently lobotomise it. ↗
