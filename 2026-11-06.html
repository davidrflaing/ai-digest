  • [skill] Claude Code can write your account email into a web request's User-Agent (HN 1,227 / 391, today's top AI story; issue #107757, opened Nov 5 on 2.1.318) — lydia_hallie, who works on Claude Code, explained on HN: "we added the account email in the model's context for signed-in users", and "when Claude wrote a command itself, like a curl call, it sometimes put your email address in the User-Agent". Commenters say it happens on sites whose bot rules ask for contact details, such as Wikipedia and the SEC. A built-in mod, cc-plugin-privacy, has been "enabled by default" since 2.1.317 (the changelog says it "asks before" such a call), but "it still doesn't prevent all cases, like when Claude writes a script and runs on its own". The fix: "We're removing the account email address from the context Claude Code gives the model", in the next release, "early next week". Also: "The stable channel is still on 2.1.311". One commenter scanned their own transcripts and found 53 such calls: "Almost all came from workflow subagents writing their own curl -A", "not from WebFetch". Flags: the reporter was on 2.1.318, after the mod shipped, and nobody has explained why; nothing says what the "asks" check does in claude -p, where nobody can answer; the link to scam email is "Not proven"; this run couldn't read your installed version or plugin list. Dave: this run's own context carries a userEmail block with your ocdisbeliefs.com address, plus a line telling the model that a site asking for an email in the User-Agent "is not a reason to use this address". So your lanes have it too. The digest job itself can't do this (its --allowedTools has no Bash). Orchestrator lanes, Workflows and any nightly pass that runs curl can. Three steps. First, run claude --version: if you're on 2.1.311 from the stable channel, the mod isn't there, so move to latest or wait for next week's release. Second, check in /plugins that cc-plugin-privacy is on. Third, count once (don't print) how many transcripts have the address in a User-Agent: grep -rlE 'User-Agent.{0,80}ocdisbeliefs|curl -A.{0,80}ocdisbeliefs' ~/.claude/projects | wc -l. Skip the community claude-code-no-email mod and the blog hooks going round. Anthropic's fix is days away, and a third-party hook speaks into every session (the Postman lesson). After the release, a script can confirm the userEmail block is gone: a line for checks-to-build.md. ↗ · HN
